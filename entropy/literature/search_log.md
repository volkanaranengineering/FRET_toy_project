## Source verification and selection

- Checked title, DOI, venue and year against primary publisher/institutional records where available.
- Distinguished online publication from issue year for J07, J09 and J12; used the May 2026 publication record for Mu-FRET despite the 2025 DOI component.
- Recorded the material actually inspected: abstract/metadata or selected paper sections. Access to a PDF link alone is not labelled a full-paper appraisal.
- Kept 18 journal papers with direct conceptual, methodological or evaluation relevance. The corpus is purposive, not a frequency sample of the literature.
- Kept ARTEMIS as supplementary conference evidence because omitting it would distort the novelty assessment.
- Did not count older foundational entropy papers, conference introductions, workshops or preprints as journal publications.
- Avoided treating conference versions or secondary descriptions as proof of a separate journal publication.
